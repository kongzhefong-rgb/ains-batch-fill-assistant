AINS Batch Fill Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Overview

AINS Batch Fill Assistant is an independent browser extension for authorised teachers who enter reading records in the Advanced Integrated NILAM System (AINS). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or provided by Malaysia's Ministry of Education, AINS, Google, or Supabase.

Information handled by the extension

The extension processes book-record information entered by the teacher, including fields required for AINS reading records. It also processes student names, optional classes, and DELIMa email addresses when a teacher imports a CSV, XLSX, or authorised Google Sheets list. This information is stored locally in Chrome extension storage on the teacher's device so the selected workflow can continue.

The extension does not collect, generate, store, or fill student Google or DELIMa passwords.

Teacher access

Teacher email addresses and passwords entered into the extension's teacher-access screen are sent directly over HTTPS to Supabase Authentication to verify access. Teacher passwords are not stored by the extension. After successful sign-in, Supabase session and refresh tokens may be retained locally in Chrome extension storage until sign-out or expiry.

Services and data transmission

The extension operates on the AINS website and may communicate with: (1) Supabase, for teacher account authentication; (2) Google Sheets, only when a teacher chooses to import an authorised sheet; and (3) Google sign-in pages, only to place the teacher-selected student's imported email address into the Google account identifier field where technically supported. The extension does not send book records or student lists to a developer-operated server.

Use and disclosure

The extension does not sell personal data, use advertising or analytics trackers, or disclose data to third parties except for the services described above when initiated by the teacher. AINS account actions and record submissions are performed on the official AINS service under the teacher's direction.

Security and retention

Teachers should use only authorised student information and protect access to their device. Teachers can remove locally stored student lists through the extension controls, sign out of teacher access, or remove the extension to clear its local extension storage.
