AINS Batch Fill Assistant – Privacy Policy

AINS Batch Fill Assistant is an independent browser utility designed to assist users with entering reading records on the AINS website.

The extension operates only on the AINS website.

The extension does not sell, transfer, or share user data with third parties.

Book-record information entered by the user is processed locally in the browser for the purpose of assisting with AINS data entry. The extension does not intentionally transmit this information to an external server or third-party analytics service.

The extension may use Chrome's local storage to retain extension settings and user preferences.

The extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially provided by the Ministry of Education Malaysia or the AINS system.

For support, please refer to the support information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
